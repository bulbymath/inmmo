Definition 2

The Noted Operation

Collapses un-noted meniners into a single harmonic sum:

$$\mathfrak{M}_{\natural}(a_1, a_2, \dots, a_n) = \left(\sum_{i=1}^{n} a_i^{-1}\right)^{-1}$$
Lemma 3

Paek2 Lemma (Flat Meniner)

Extracts an individual rate from a cooperative system:

$$\mathfrak{M}_{\flat}(a, T) = \frac{aT}{a - T}$$
